Session & Security Tokens

Cookie Policy

How StudentXces uses essential authentication cookies, CSRF defense tokens, and browser session storage to secure campus portals without third-party advertising tracking.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Version: 2.4 • Advertising Trackers Used: 0 (Zero)
No Commercial Tracking Cookies
StudentXces uses cookies strictly for system security, user session persistence, and role-based portal authorization. We do not embed ad network pixels, social media tracking beacons, or cross-site user behavioral scripts.

1 What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

A cookie is a compact text string stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) by your web browser when visiting web applications. Similar technologies include HTML5 local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage), which allow web applications to securely store session state and interface preferences locally on your browser.

Because StudentXces hosts mission-critical educational databases (including examination marks and financial fee challans), our platform relies on secure, encrypted cookies to authenticate staff and prevent malicious session hijacking.

2 Cookies & Local Tokens Utilized

The following table outlines all cookies and browser storage keys deployed across StudentXces domains:

3 Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Many consumer platforms allow third-party behavioral ad trackers. StudentXces explicitly prohibits ad-tech cookies. Specifically:

  • No Facebook / Meta retargeting pixels are embedded in school portals.
  • No Google Display Network or third-party ad bidding trackers are present.
  • No cross-site tracking across student or parent online activity is conducted.

4 Managing Cookie Settings in Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow users to inspect, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. For instructions, consult the documentation of your browser:

  • Google Chrome (Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data)
  • Microsoft Edge (Settings > Cookies and site permissions)
  • Apple Safari (Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies)
  • Mozilla Firefox (Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection)

Important Notice: Because our cookies are classified as strictly necessary for role authentication and CSRF security, blocking or deleting StudentXces cookies will prevent campus staff, parents, and principals from securely logging into their dashboards.
