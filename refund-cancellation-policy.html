Refund & Cancellation Policy

Guidelines governing subscription renewals, 14-day onboarding guarantee, cancellation timelines, and prorated refund eligibility for subscribing schools.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Document Version: 2.4 • Currency: Pakistani Rupees (PKR)
14-Day Campus Onboarding Guarantee
We want school leadership to experience complete confidence in StudentXces. If, within the first 14 days of activation, our engineering team cannot align the system to your school's required 3-part challan layout or grading rules, we provide a 100% full refund of your subscription fee.

1 Transparent Pricing & Billing Schedules

StudentXces is provided as a SaaS subscription billed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Subscriptions are available on quarterly or annual billing cycles with transparent student and staff capacity allocations:

  • Core: PKR 3,000 / month (billed quarterly at PKR 9,000, or annually at PKR 32,400 with a 10% discount) supporting up to 100 students and 25 staff accounts.
  • Growth: PKR 5,000 / month (billed quarterly at PKR 15,000, or annually at PKR 54,000 with a 10% discount) supporting up to 200 students and 50 staff accounts.
  • Enterprise: PKR 8,000 / month (billed quarterly at PKR 24,000, or annually at PKR 86,400 with a 10% discount) supporting up to 400 students and 75 staff accounts.

2 14-Day Onboarding Guarantee Terms

The 14-day onboarding evaluation window commences on the date your school’s administrative credentials are issued and initial student database migration is completed.

To request a refund under the guarantee, the School Principal or designated billing authority must submit a written request to support@studentxces.io or via our verified WhatsApp Helpline stating the technical or workflow incompatibility encountered. Upon verification, the full subscription amount paid is reimbursed via direct inter-bank transfer (IBFT) within 5 business days.

3 Cancellation Procedure & Data Handover

Schools may cancel their subscription at any time by providing written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the start of the next billing cycle.

Upon receipt of a cancellation notice:

  • Your school retains uninterrupted portal access through the end of the paid billing period.
  • A dedicated 30-day data export period begins, enabling administrative staff to download comprehensive Excel/CSV archives containing all student biodata, attendance records, past fee ledgers, and exam marks.
  • Our engineering team assists with bulk export requests at zero additional charge.

4 Refund Eligibility Summary

Subscription Scenario Refund Eligibility Conditions & Calculation
Within 14-day onboarding window 100% Full Refund Applies to all new school subscriptions where technical incompatibility is identified.
Quarterly billing (after 14 days) Non-refundable Access remains active through the remainder of the 3-month cycle. Next quarter is cancelled.
Annual billing mid-cycle cancellation Prorated Quarterly Refund Unconsumed remaining full calendar quarters refunded, recalculating used quarters at standard non-discounted rate.
Third-party SMS / WhatsApp Credits Non-refundable Third-party telecom telecommunication units consumed for parent SMS/WhatsApp broadcasts are non-recoverable.

5 Capacity Expansion & Upgrades

As schools enroll additional students (e.g. growing past 100 students on Core or 200 on Growth), campus administrators can immediately upgrade to a higher tier.

When upgrading mid-cycle, the unconsumed balance of your current billing period is credited towards the upgraded tier. There is zero disruption to campus operations, challan numbering sequences, or parent portal logins during plan migration.

6 Grace Period & Account Suspension Rules

We recognize that school fee collections peak at specific times of the academic calendar. If a renewal invoice is unpaid upon the due date, StudentXces provides a 15-day active grace period during which campus portals, bank challan printing, and parent messaging continue without restriction.

Following the 15-day grace period, if payment has not been settled and no payment plan is agreed upon, portal write-privileges will be temporarily paused. Student and historical records remain fully preserved and protected.
