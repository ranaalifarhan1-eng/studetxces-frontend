Student & Campus Data Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

How StudentXces collects, safeguards, and isolates student records, parent communications, academic transcripts, and institutional financial data.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Document Version: 2.4 • Governing Jurisdiction: [Punjab, Islamic Republic of Pakistan]
Our Fundamental Student Data Pledge
StudentXces operates as an enterprise software partner for educational institutions. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or expose student, teacher, or guardian information to third-party advertisers. The subscribing school retains complete ownership of all academic, biometric/attendance, and fee transaction records at all times.

1 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which StudentXces Technologies Pakistan (“StudentXces”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) processes, stores, and protects data collected through the StudentXces School Management ERP platform, associated role-based portals (Principal, Teacher, Accountant, Parent, and Student), and related web services.

In the context of data protection principles, the educational institution contracting our software (“School” or “Client”) serves as the Data Controller, determining the purpose and categories of student and administrative records processed. StudentXces operates solely as the Data Processor, processing records strictly under the instruction of the school and within the parameters of our service subscription agreement.

2 Categories of Information Processed

To deliver comprehensive school automation and campus management, the platform processes the following institutional data sets provided by the school administration, staff, and guardians:

Student & Enrollment Data

Full name, roll number, admission number, date of birth, gender, class/section allocation, blood group, guardian names, CNIC/B-Form reference numbers, and residence address.

Financial & Fee Records

Monthly tuition structures, concession categories, 3-part bank challan serials, KuickPay / 1Link transaction references, bank branch stamp stamps, and outstanding arrears ledger.

Attendance & Academics

Daily roll call timestamps, subject test marks, midterm/final examination grading sheets, teacher remarks, homework submissions, and student conduct evaluations.

Staff & Operational Data

Faculty profiles, designation, assigned course timetable, monthly salary slips, leave balances, and administrative role permissions.

3 Purpose of Processing

Data provided to StudentXces is processed strictly for the execution of campus operations, including:

  • Generation and reconciliation of official 3-part bank fee challans compatible with Pakistani commercial banks.
  • Dispatch of automated WhatsApp and SMS attendance and fee reminder notices to verified guardian mobile numbers.
  • Real-time grade compilation, report card printing, and performance analytics for school leadership.
  • Maintaining tenant-segregated access for teachers, accountants, principals, students, and parents.
  • Providing technical audit logs to detect unauthorized access attempts or suspicious configuration updates.

4 Zero Advertising & Non-Commercialization Guarantee

Unlike consumer applications, StudentXces is a paid institutional B2B software platform. We adhere to an absolute prohibition against commercial data mining:

  • We do NOT sell, lease, transfer, or disclose school or student data to data brokers or advertising agencies.
  • We do NOT display third-party advertisements or promotional banners inside any student or parent portal.
  • We do NOT build commercial behavioral profiles or track student browsing history outside the ERP platform.

5 Authorized Subprocessors & Service Infrastructure

To deliver uninterrupted 99.98% cloud uptime and automated communication, StudentXces utilizes selected enterprise-grade subprocessors under strict confidentiality obligations:

Subprocessor Category Purpose Data Transferred
Cloud Hosting & Database Encrypted database storage, high availability compute nodes, and automated snapshots. Full institutional ERP database (AES-256 encrypted at rest).
WhatsApp & SMS Gateway Dispatch of fee reminders, attendance alerts, and exam notices to parents. Recipient mobile phone number and notification message body only.
Fintech / 1Link Invoicing Digital fee challan reconciliation and bill inquiry verification for partnered banks. Student challan consumer ID, due date, and billed tuition amount.

6 Minors’ Privacy & Parental Access

Given that school records inevitably involve minor students, StudentXces applies stringent protective measures aligned with international best practices:

Parents and legal guardians access their children’s attendance, fee receipts, and marks strictly through individualized, encrypted parent credentials. In the event a parent wishes to review, modify, or correct personal student information, such requests must be routed directly to the administration of their respective school, which maintains ultimate authority to edit student enrollment rosters.

7 Data Retention & Institutional Offboarding

StudentXces retains institutional data for the active duration of the school’s subscription agreement. Upon formal cancellation or termination of services:

  • The school administration is granted a 30-day export window to download all student lists, fee ledgers, and academic histories in standard Excel / CSV formats free of charge.
  • Following the expiration of the handover period, all active database records, cached media, and tenant indices are permanently purged in accordance with secure data destruction standards.
  • Encrypted historical disaster-recovery backups are rotated out and irrevocably overwritten within our standard 60-day backup cycle.
