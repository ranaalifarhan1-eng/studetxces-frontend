Institutional Subscription Overview
By activating a campus subscription or accessing the StudentXces portals, the educational institution agrees to these terms. StudentXces grants a non-exclusive, cloud-hosted SaaS license tailored for campus administration, fee billing, and student records management.

1 Institutional Authority & Acceptance

These Terms & Conditions constitute a legally binding agreement between StudentXces Technologies Pakistan (“StudentXces”, “Provider”) and the subscribing educational entity (“School”, “Client”, or “Subscriber”).

The individual executing registration or signing the onboarding form represents and warrants that they possess legitimate legal authority (as School Principal, Managing Director, Trustee, or designated campus administrator) to bind the institution to this agreement.

2 Software License Grant & Portal Privileges

Subject to timely payment of subscription dues, StudentXces grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access and utilize the StudentXces Cloud ERP platform. Access is structured through role-segregated portals:

  • Principal / Executive Dashboard: Campus-wide oversight, financial collections, staff approval, and overall operational reporting.
  • Accounts & Cashier Portal: Fee structure generation, 3-part bank challan printing, discount approvals, and expense tracking.
  • Teacher & Staff Portal: Daily attendance logging, subject marks entry, syllabus progress, and guardian notes.
  • Parent & Student Portal: Fee status checks, digital receipts, exam report cards, and campus announcements.

4 Client Responsibilities & Credential Hygiene

The Client institution agrees to uphold administrative integrity:

  • Ensure staff members maintain confidentiality of login credentials and enforce prompt deactivation for departed faculty.
  • Review fee structures and voucher templates before executing bulk print runs to prevent calculation mismatches.
  • Adhere to applicable local education board guidelines and avoid inputting fraudulent academic grades.
  • Refrain from attempting reverse engineering, unauthorized penetration probing, or reselling portal credentials.

5 Service Availability & 99.98% Cloud SLA

StudentXces provides an uptime service level target of 99.98% availability for core portal endpoints.

Planned system maintenance, software patches, and database indexing are scheduled during low-traffic overnight hours (typically 01:00 AM – 04:00 AM PKT). In the event of emergency server maintenance, registered campus IT directors are notified via WhatsApp broadcast.

All software code, user interface designs, algorithms, Challan layout templates, database architectures, and trademarks associated with StudentXces remain the exclusive intellectual property of Provider.

The Client retains full ownership of its school trademarks, crests, campus logos, and proprietary syllabi uploaded to the platform. By uploading campus branding, Client grants Provider the limited right to render these assets across portals, printed vouchers, and parent notification headers.

7 Subscription Duration, Cancellation & Suspension

Subscriptions renew automatically at the conclusion of each billing quarter or annual cycle unless cancelled in writing with 30 days notice prior to renewal.

In the event of an outstanding subscription invoice, a 15-day grace period is provided during which portal access remains active. If payment remains unsettled after the grace period, administrative write-access may be restricted while retaining data download capabilities.
