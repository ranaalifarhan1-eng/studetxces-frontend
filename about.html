Who We Are

Our Story & Origin

For years, private schools across Pakistan have struggled with virus-prone offline desktop software, manual registers, and tedious monthly fee challan distribution. StudentXces was created to replace fragile offline computers with a secure, centralized cloud portal accessible anytime by administrators, teachers, and parents.

StudentXces is built and owned by Pakalfa, a technology company founded in 2019 and based in Lahore. Pakalfa provides domain registration, web hosting, SSL certificates and website tools to businesses, backed by 24/7 customer support. Drawing on this experience in running reliable online infrastructure, Pakalfa created StudentXces to bring the same dependable, always-available cloud technology to schools across Pakistan.
